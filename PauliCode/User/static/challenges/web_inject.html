The Login Obelisk

📜 The Story

You arrive at the gates of Mr. Leo's Dungeon Arena, a massive stone structure covered in glowing runes. Mr. Leo greets you silently from the shadows — his eyes sharp, his cloak flickering with stealth magic.

He reveals the next obstacle: an ancient Login Obelisk created by King Kharl.

The Obelisk displays a glowing message:

"ONLY TRUE HEROES MAY ENTER. PROVIDE VALID CREDENTIALS."

A username and password field float magically in the air… but Mr. Leo leans close and whispers:

"King Kharl enchanted this obelisk badly… It trusts its own inputs a little too much."

He smirks. You realize the obelisk is vulnerable — if this is a login, perhaps it can be tricked.

⚔️ The door rumbles, waiting for an attempt. ⚔️
